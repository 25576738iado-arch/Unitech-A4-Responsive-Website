Programs and participation

Choose a pathway that matches your goals.

01

Student Digital Confidence

This program helps students strengthen everyday digital skills, from online communication and safe browsing to managing files, researching information, and using collaborative tools with confidence.

02

Careers and Study Readiness

Designed for learners preparing for their next learning or work step, this pathway focusses on planning, communication, self-management, and goal setting. Students build confidence through guided activities and short mentoring sessions.

03

Family Learning Connect

Parents and carers are invited to join short online sessions that explain how to support digital learning at home. Topics include routines, wellbeing, online safety, and how to communicate effectively with schools and tutors.

Simple four-step process

How to participate

  1. Review the programs and identify the one that best matches your interests or goals.
  2. Complete the enquiry form on the contact page so we can understand your needs.
  3. Receive a welcome email with session details, timing information, and next steps.
  4. Join the online group, attend the first session, and begin building confidence with support.
