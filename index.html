Supporting learners beyond the classroom

Helping students and families thrive through online outreach.

Unitech Online Outreach Community connects young people with practical support, confidence-building activities, and trusted guidance that helps them prepare for study, work, and everyday digital life.

We are designed for students, parents, and carers who want a warm, approachable way to build skills, ask questions, and join a welcoming learning community without needing to travel to a campus.

Who we support

Our outreach community is built for students aged 12 to 18, as well as the parents and carers who want to understand how digital learning can feel more accessible and empowering at home.

Whether a learner needs encouragement with confidence, guidance on study planning, or an introduction to online community activities, our programs are designed to reduce barriers and create a sense of belonging.

What to expect

Every program begins with a welcoming introduction, clear goals, and a simple plan for participation. Sessions are practical, inclusive, and focused on helping participants feel informed and supported at each stage.

Explore our current offerings or contact the team to discuss which program is the best fit for your child or family circumstances.
